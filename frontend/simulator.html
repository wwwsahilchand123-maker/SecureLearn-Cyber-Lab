💡 What to Look For in These Simulations

🔗 Check the URL

  • Look for misspellings in domain
  • Verify HTTPS and certificate
  • Check for unusual subdomains
  • Watch for IP addresses

🎨 Visual Clues

  • Poor quality logos
  • Inconsistent branding
  • Spelling/grammar errors
  • Generic design elements

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Urgent action required
  • Account suspension threats
  • Too-good-to-be-true offers
  • Unexpected requests

🔒 Security Features

  • Missing security badges
  • No multi-factor auth option
  • Unusual login requirements
  • Lack of contact information
